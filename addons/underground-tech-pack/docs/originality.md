# Originality and decorative-only rules

These rules implement the [Crew Brief](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WGthGOh2_vrdjSrjwmkx6YfAtmFI9gC1AgpEYUUmZ-k/edit).

- All assets must be original and unbranded.
- Do not reproduce third-party superhero names, logos, symbols, characters, vehicles, costumes, architecture, or recognizable prop designs.
- Support the broad fantasy of a secret underground technology headquarters through an original industrial identity.
- Do not copy recognizable screen graphics, control layouts, secret-door designs, furniture, vehicles, or other signature franchise elements.
- Future displays must use original abstract graphics. When uncertain, redesign toward generic industrial or original Underground Tech Pack language.
- Keep the free edition decorative-only. Do not add interactive screens, animated doors, sitting behavior, powered machinery, scripted hidden entrances, or advanced pipe logic.

The palette and pixel grid were authored directly for this project. No third-party textures, logos, screen layouts, or reference imagery were imported. The prototype is a generic framed navy panel with four small fasteners.

The optional lab function is development tooling, invoked manually by the tester and excluded from the player bundle. It is not a gameplay feature. Do not add ticking functions or runtime scripting.
